Add `ContextMenu` and `Sheet` to `@strapi/design-system/next`, and range styling to `Calendar`.

`ContextMenu` opens a menu at the pointer on a right-click, with the parts and the look of the `DropdownMenu`. `Sheet`
is a panel that slides in from an edge of the screen, modal or not, with a `responsive` side (a bottom sheet on small
screens, a right rail from `medium` up) and a `floating` card look. `Calendar` with `mode="range"` now marks the ends of
the range and the band between them.
